Free apps and softwares

Take a look at the Free Software Foundation fsf.org

A guide to De-Googlization on videoteca.kenobit.it (ita).

Google is limiting the freedom of Android: read keepandroidopen.org and sign the petition!

Examples of alternative softwares:

  • Communications: Matrix matrix.org, an open protocol for decentralised, secure communications
  • Social: Fediverse fediverso.it
  • Install non-Google apps on Android: f-droid.org
  • Upload Videos: joinpeertube.org
  • Search engine: SearXNG, installation here github.com/searxng/searxng, and an instance here clippy.kenobit.it
  • Email: Protonmail, Tutanota, kSuite, a personal website, ...
  • Video calls: Jitsi Meet; an instance is vc.autistici.org
  • Drive, form, shared documents, chat & videocall: Nextcloud nextcloud.com, but you need to rent/host a server (advanced)
  • Browser: Fennec (Android) and Librewolf, Ungoogled Chromium (Desktop)
  • Maps: Organic Maps organicmaps.app, an interface to Open Street Maps
    Contribute to the maps via StreetComplete
  • Videos: NewPipe newpipe.net (youtube w/o ads & w/ playback background)
  • Music: Overtune outertune.org (a client taking youtube music with spotify-like interface) or Harmony music harmony-music.org, or FunkWhale funkwhale.audio
  • Podcasts: AntennaPod antennapod.org or CastoPod castopod.org
  • Reading and reviewing: BookWyrm joinbookwyrm.com
  • Newsletter: ListMonk listmonk.app
  • Final boss: limit Google Play Services by using a Custom ROM like GrapheneOS (solo telefono Pixel; limita l'attività di GPS) oppure /e/OS, LineageOS, CalyxOS (utilizzano una riscrittura libera di GPS)
