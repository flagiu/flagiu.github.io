# Codies

Most projects in ```ipynb/``` are inspired by [profconradi.com](https://profconradi.com) .

Most projects in ```py/``` and in ```pyodide/``` are copied or inspired by [gitlab.com/uduh/physics-simulations](https://gitlab.com/uduh/physics-simulations) .
